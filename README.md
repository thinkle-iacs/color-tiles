# Color Tiles

A one-day Web Design 2 exercise.

We are creating a class set of square designs to print in color and display together. Each tile will be **6 inches × 6 inches**.

Our class palette is **red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple**. There are three students, so each student will create **two tiles**, one for each of their two assigned colors. Coordinate assignments so the class covers all six colors once.

## The challenge

Let your assigned color inspire the emotion of each design. Choose your **topic, colors, typography, and image** based on that emotion. What does this color make you feel or think about? How can every part of the design express that feeling?

Use shades and tints of your assigned color, with supporting colors as needed for contrast and readability. Your assigned color should be the clear focus. Your two tiles can explore different topics and moods.

## Get started

1. Run `npm start`. In Codespaces, open port **5500** from the Ports tab to preview your two tiles. Locally, open `http://localhost:5500`.
2. Replace the starter text and add an image to each tile. Save image files in this repo and use relative paths, such as `images/my-photo.jpg`, with descriptive `alt` text.
3. Edit `styles.css`. Change `.tile-one` and `.tile-two` to match your assigned colors, then design each tile's layout and typography. Add your own classes as needed. Keep the `.tile` dimensions at 6 inches square.
4. Check both designs in the browser and in print preview. Keep all text and images inside the squares.

**Finish both tiles in one class period.** Spend a few minutes choosing your ideas, most of the period designing, and the last few minutes checking your work and pushing your changes.

## Before you finish

- Two finished square designs, one for each assigned color.
- Each tile includes a topic, intentional typography, and an image that reflect the emotion you associate with its color.
- Text is readable, images are clear, and nothing is accidentally cut off.
- Your changes are committed and pushed.

## Printing

Print `index.html` on Letter paper in portrait orientation at **100% / Actual size**. Turn on **background graphics** and turn off browser headers and footers. Avoid “Fit to page,” which can change the tile size.

The CSS sets each square to 6 inches × 6 inches for print. Its apparent size on a screen depends on your display and browser zoom. Trim the printed pages to the edges of the squares for the class display.
